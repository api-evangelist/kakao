Kakao and Samsung jointly hosted their first AI hackathon, the SSAFY x Kakao Tech Bootcamp AI Hackathon, held June 13-14 at Kakao's AI Campus in Yongin. The event brought together participants from Kakao Tech Bootcamp and Samsung's SSAFY youth software/AI academy to go beyond coding competitions and address real-world problems using AI.
